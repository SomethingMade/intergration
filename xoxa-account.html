You can request deletion of your Xoxa account and associated data at any time. This page explains how to request deletion, and what happens to your data afterward.

Last updated August 15, 2026

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. In-app (recommended): Open Xoxa, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account, confirm your phone number, and follow the on-screen steps to permanently delete your account.
  2. By email: If you can't access the app, send an email to lordmade100@gmail.com from the address associated with your account (or including your registered phone number), with the subject line "Account Deletion Request." Include your registered phone number so we can locate your account.

This action is permanent. Once your account is deleted, it cannot be recovered.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are typically processed within a few business days. You'll receive a confirmation once your account and associated data have been removed.
