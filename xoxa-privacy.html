Privacy Policy.

This explains what information Xoxa collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Xoxa, you agree to the practices described here.

Last updated August 15, 2026

Xoxa ("we", "our", "the app") is a messaging and social application developed by Vynix. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Xoxa, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Phone number — used to create your account and verify your identity via a one-time password (OTP).
  • Profile information — display name, username, profile photo, and status/bio you choose to add.
  • Account type — whether your account is set as a personal or Business Account.

Messages and Media

  • Text messages, voice notes, photos, videos, and view-once media sent within chats and groups.
  • Timestamps, delivery status, and read receipts.
  • Group information, including group membership, Business Page details, and content posted to group/Business Pages.

Status, Feed and Discover Content

  • Videos, photos, text posts and captions you upload to your Status or Feed.
  • Interactions such as likes, comments and reshares on Status or Feed content.
  • Basic view/impression counts on your own posts, shown to you in Account Insights.

Calls

  • Metadata about voice calls (1:1 and group), such as call duration and participants. Call audio is transmitted directly between participants (peer-to-peer) and is not stored by us.

Device and Push Notification Data

  • Device push token, used to deliver notifications through our push provider (OneSignal).
  • Basic device/app information (OS version, app version) for troubleshooting and compatibility.

Payment Information

  • If you purchase a verified badge or other paid feature, payment is processed by our third-party payment provider (Yoco). We do not store your card details — only the transaction status and purchase record.

2. Business Accounts & Ads

Xoxa lets any account switch to a Business Account. Ads on Xoxa are created and published by Business Accounts themselves through the post composer — we do not run a separate ad-serving network, and we do not sell your data to advertisers.

  • Ad posts — a Business Account can label a post as an Ad and attach a destination link and call-to-action button (e.g. "Learn More", "Shop Now"). These posts are shown in the Feed alongside regular posts and are clearly marked "Ad".
  • Destination links — the link behind an Ad's call-to-action button is set by the Business Account that created it, not by us. Tapping it takes you to a third-party site outside Xoxa; we don't control that site's content, privacy practices, or security.
  • Business Page content — converting a group to a Business Page, and any content, username, or badge associated with it, is visible to anyone who can view that page.
  • Post performance — Business Accounts can see aggregate view counts for their own posts and Ads in Account Insights. We don't provide advertisers with data about who specifically viewed or clicked a post.
  • No cross-app ad tracking — we don't use third-party ad networks, ad trackers, or cross-app/cross-site tracking to target ads to you within Xoxa.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account, and verify you via OTP.
  • To deliver messages, media, calls, and notifications between you and other users.
  • To operate features such as read receipts, group management, Status, Feed and Discover.
  • To display Ads that Business Accounts publish, and to show Business Accounts aggregate performance for their own posts.
  • To process purchases (e.g. verified badges) via our payment provider.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the app, including diagnosing bugs.
  • To communicate with you about your account or important service updates.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Data is stored using Firebase infrastructure with industry-standard security practices.
  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • View-once media is deleted after being viewed, consistent with its intended purpose.
  • We retain account, message, and post data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the service.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access & correction — you can view and edit your profile information within the app.
  • Business Account — you can switch between a personal and Business Account at any time; the app will warn you about what changes before you switch back.
  • Blocking — you can block or unblock other users at any time.
  • Deletion — you may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. See how to delete your account for step-by-step instructions.
  • Notifications — you can disable push notifications through your device settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Xoxa is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children under this age, and Ads on Xoxa are not directed at children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time — for example, as Ads or Business Account features change. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when changes are made. Continued use of Xoxa after changes means you accept the updated policy.
