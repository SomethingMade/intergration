WHAT IS HABA?

More than a chat app.

Haba brings conversations and social sharing together. Chat privately, communicate in groups, share photos and videos, post stories, discover content and express yourself through GIFs, music and voice notes.

EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE

Built around the way you connect.

Haba gives you different ways to communicate, share and interact without making everything feel complicated.

Private Messaging

Have direct conversations with the people you connect with. Keep your chats simple, personal and easy to follow.

Voice Notes

Sometimes talking is easier than typing. Record and send voice notes directly in your conversations.

Photos & Videos

Share photos and videos in your conversations and group chats to keep people involved in the moment.

Groups

Bring multiple people together in group conversations and share messages and media with everyone at once.

Stories

Share moments through stories and interact with the people you follow through likes, replies and resharing.

Social Feed

Discover posts and video content from the Haba community and interact with what you find interesting.

Music

Add music to your content and make the things you share feel more personal.

GIFs & More

Express yourself beyond words with GIFs and other creative media while chatting or creating content.

Notifications

Stay updated when something happens around your conversations and social activity.
