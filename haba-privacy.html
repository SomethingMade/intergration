Privacy Policy

This explains what information Haba collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. Last updated 19 September 2026.

1. Information we collect

To create and run your Haba account, and to provide features such as messaging and ride sharing, we collect the information below.

  • Account details — your phone number (used for OTP sign-in), display name, avatar and any profile info you add.
  • Content you share — messages, photos, videos, voice notes, documents, stories, feed posts and comments you send or post.
  • Usage data — device type, app version, crash logs and general interaction data used to keep Haba working reliably.
  • Push notification tokens — used only to deliver notifications for messages, likes, comments and other activity.
  • Location data — when you use ride sharing, your device location (to set your pickup point, find nearby drivers and follow your trip), plus the pickup and destination addresses you enter.
  • Ride details — your ride requests, the route, fare and time estimates, trip status and timestamps, the driver assigned to your trip, and any support requests about a ride.
  • Payment data — if you pay for a ride, buy a verified badge or use another paid feature, payment is processed by our payment providers; Haba does not store your card details.

2. How your content is stored

Account data, chats and social content are stored on secure cloud infrastructure. Photos, videos, voice notes and other media you share are stored on secure media hosting so they load quickly across devices.

3. Private messages

One-to-one direct messages support end-to-end encryption: message text and media are encrypted on your device before they're sent, and only you and the person you're messaging can decrypt them. Group chats, the AI assistant thread, GIFs and stickers are not end-to-end encrypted. View-once media is deleted after it's viewed and is not kept in your chat history.

4. Ride sharing and your location

Haba lets you request rides and connect with drivers. To make that work, we handle some information that is more sensitive than the rest of the app, so here is exactly how it's used.

  • Location — we use your location to set your pickup point, show your position on the map, find available drivers and follow the trip. Location access depends on the permission you give Haba on your device, and you can change it at any time in your device settings. Ride features that need your location may not work fully if you turn it off.
  • What drivers see — once you request a ride, the driver can see your pickup and destination, your display name and your trip progress. You'll see your driver's name, vehicle details and their location during the trip. Drivers don't get access to your chats, contacts or feed.
  • Maps and dispatch — your pickup and destination addresses and route are processed by our mapping providers to search addresses, draw the route and estimate fare and arrival time. Your ride request is passed to our dispatch and driver-management provider so a driver can be assigned and the trip tracked.
  • Safety and disputes — we may use ride details to investigate safety incidents, resolve fare or trip disputes, prevent fraud and respond to support requests.
  • Your messages stay separate — ride sharing doesn't change how your private messages are protected, and we don't use the contents of your end-to-end encrypted chats to provide rides.

5. How we use your information

  • To operate core features — messaging, calls, stories, groups and the social feed.
  • To provide ride sharing — connecting you with drivers, showing routes and trip progress, and calculating fares and arrival times.
  • To send notifications about activity relevant to you.
  • To maintain safety and security, including blocking and abuse prevention.
  • To improve reliability and fix problems through crash and performance data.
  • To process payments for rides and optional paid features.
  • To investigate ride safety incidents and disputes, and to prevent fraud.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Sharing your information

We share information only where necessary to run Haba: with our cloud hosting and media storage providers, our payment processors for purchases, and push notification services to deliver alerts. For ride sharing, we also share the details needed to complete your trip with your driver, and with our mapping and dispatch providers, as described in section 4. We may disclose ride or account information if the law requires it or to protect the safety of riders, drivers or others. Content you post publicly — such as feed posts, stories or comments — is visible to other users according to your privacy settings.

7. Your choices

  • Edit or remove your profile information at any time in Settings.
  • Turn off push notifications from your device settings.
  • Turn location access on or off for Haba from your device settings.
  • Block or report other users directly from a chat or profile.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data — see the Account page.

8. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your profile and content within a reasonable period, except where we're required to retain limited records for legal or safety reasons. Ride records, such as trip details and payment records, may be kept for longer where needed for safety, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, accounting or legal obligations.

9. Children's privacy

Haba is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll update the date at the top of this page when changes are made.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to support@webmessenger.app.
