YOUR ACCOUNT

Manage your Haba account.

Sign-in, profile and account deletion for Haba are handled in the app. This page explains how, and lets you request account or data deletion if you no longer have the app installed.

IN THE APP

Signing in

Haba uses your phone number to sign in — enter your number and confirm the 6-digit code sent to you by SMS. No password is required.

Phone number sign-in Settings → Account
Edit profile & avatar Settings → Profile
App Lock (PIN / biometric) Settings → Security
Notification preferences Settings → Notifications
DATA

Your data

Your messages, media and profile information are tied to your account. Private one-to-one chats are end-to-end encrypted, so only you and the person you're messaging can read them.

Request a copy of your data
DELETE

Delete your account

You can delete your account directly from the app under Settings → Account → Delete Account. This removes your profile, messages, posts and media within a reasonable period. If you no longer have the app installed, you can request deletion here instead.
